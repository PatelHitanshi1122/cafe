About Moon Cafe

Moon Cafe is a cozy family-friendly café that combines the rich flavors of South India with popular Italian dishes. Whether you're craving crispy dosas, fluffy idlis, creamy pasta, or wood-fired pizza, our chefs prepare every meal with passion and fresh ingredients. Our warm ambiance, delicious food, and excellent service make Moon Cafe the perfect place to enjoy breakfast, lunch, dinner, or coffee with family and friends.
